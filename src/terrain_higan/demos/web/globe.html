An Earth-sized generated planet. Enter Walk below 20 km above sea level. The complete scene is prepared in all directions before movement begins, with z14s detail near the entry point and progressively coarser terrain across the 20 km walking area and distant scenery. Visibility uses spherical Earth geometry, including both your elevation above sea level and each terrain feature's elevation, without atmospheric refraction. The camera stays 2 m above the rendered ground, including the seabed, and moves at no more than 50 m/s. W/Up and S/Down move forward/back; A/Left and D/Right strafe. Release keys to stop. Mouse position looks and steers; click the view to free or bind mouse steering. Looking up/down does not change height. Wheel and +/− change speed. Space pauses or resumes controls; Escape pauses. Movement stops 20 km from the starting spot. Exit and re-enter to prepare another area. Export terrain GLB downloads the detailed 20 km area in local meter coordinates, including complete boundary tiles but excluding the distant backdrop. Exiting walk restores orbit clearance above water or terrain. Distances follow great circles and ignore terrain height. Elevation reports the generated seabed beneath water. Statistics sample the visible surface.
